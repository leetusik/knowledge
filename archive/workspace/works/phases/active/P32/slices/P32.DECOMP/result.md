**Why research first.** The plan's reason holds. The build's shape depends on two things that need finding
out, not guessing: how a research team is best run, and where it can run. That covers the agents, their briefs,
the dossier format, the single-document pipeline and the book package. Cutting the build now would fix those
shapes too early.

**Why I split the method slice.** The plan proposed one S1 covering Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, STORM/Co-STORM,
SAFE/FActScore, citation faithfulness and source credibility. Those are two separate literatures:
- **Orchestration:** how a research team is planned, briefed, fanned out, and how its gap rounds are stopped.
- **Evidence:** atomic-claim verification, attribution and citation faithfulness, source credibility, and
  checking a text against its evidence.

The dossier and ledger format is a clean interface between them. One executor opening every page of both, and
quoting the supporting passage of each, would have to thin one side to fit. That runs against "very
thoroughly" and "best quality over efficiency". Research slices already average 2.8 spans per slice on the meter
(`dispatch-summary`: 30.6 span-min and $11.05 per slice), which suggests they run long.

The extra slice costs about $11 and 30 span-min at the mean. S2 reads S1's topic, then closes with one page
that combines both halves into the recommended pipeline, so DECOMP2 gets a single design and not two halves.
S3 runs last, so it can judge each host against the actual recommended team, not an imagined one.

  from there (noted for the rehearsal slices).
- **Cutting the build now.** Rejected for the plan's reason above.
