Component — Row card, every tone (provisional values)

A card is a side-by-side text item in a figure row: a tone's soft ground, the title in the display face in the tone's ink, the body in the interface face at the column's small step, in ink. The title always says what the card is, so the tone is never the only signal. The 13 tones use 03-diagrams' names; their values here are PROVISIONAL, pending 03-diagrams. Every text measures 4.5:1 or more on its ground (result.md).
