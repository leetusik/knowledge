Foundations — The diagram tones (proposed)

Thirteen tone names, drawn from six hues and two neutrals. Four hues are the system’s own: teal is its accent, bronze and brick its idle and revoked statuses, plum its plum ink. Two are new, proposed here: olive for ok and slate for store, because the system has neither. Every tint is new, and so are the light muted line and each dark and sepia value the system does not carry. A tone gives a shape three values: a line for strokes, edges and bars, an ink for text set in the tone, and a tint for fills. Text inside a shape stays in the page’s ink. A tone is never the only sign of anything: every toned node, edge, bar and line also carries a label, a pattern or a dash.
