Component — The document’s own look under each reader theme

The reader never restyles the document. The bars take the reader’s theme; the frame’s color-scheme picks between a self-darkening document’s own two looks (Dark, or System on a dark device, shows its dark look; Light and Sepia its light one). A light-only document stays light in every theme. The kb-border hairlines above and below the frame are the seam.
