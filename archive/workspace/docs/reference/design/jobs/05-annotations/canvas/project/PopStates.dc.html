Component — Highlight popover

What a tap on a highlight, a marker or a margin note opens: the note, Review this, and delete, in that order. Editing is plain text with line breaks kept. A highlight’s card follows Review this: on makes it a card in your reviews; off pauses the card and keeps its history; deleting the highlight deletes its card, and the line under Delete says so before you press it.
