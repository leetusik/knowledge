Component — Highlights in the text

The paint is drawn as markup over the chapter’s own text. A highlight is a warm tint with a 1px line in the bronze ink along its foot, so it is never told by colour alone; a highlight with a note takes that line at 2px; where two overlap the tint goes one step deeper; the one whose popover is open takes the accent underline; text being selected takes the accent’s pale tint until it is saved. Below 64rem a block’s markers sit under its heading (its note, Text changed, another language) and "Bookmarked" sits above the heading, in its margin, so no text moves. Hover (the 2px bronze line on any highlight) is reached on the Pages.
