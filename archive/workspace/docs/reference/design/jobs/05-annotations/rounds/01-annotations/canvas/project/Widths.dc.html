The reader with its marks, at the four reference sizes

One prototype state drawn four times. A click in any frame moves all four. Each frame carries the state bar at the top of its page, so it scrolls away with the chapter; a scene, theme or text size picked in any frame shows in all four. From 1024 the notes move into the margin. The book's list (Contents, then Notes, then All notes in this book →) and the legacy chapter (Another book · Contents, then Datums) open by a click in any frame, and the bar then shows their own states.
