Ratings, Next and interval previews

The foot of every card. Graded types (choice, cloze) rate through Next, which says before it is pressed what it will send; recall and highlights rate with two buttons, each with its interval preview. Saving and failing change the words and the label, never the size. Intervals read as “in 10 minutes”, “in 1 day”, “in 23 days”, “in 2 months”, “in 1.3 years” (open question 4).
