- **THE ACCEPTED `?next=` SHAPE — `P28.S6` MUST MATCH THIS.** `web/src/lib/next-path.ts` exports

  resolving); a repeated `?next=` (arrives as `string[]`); the empty value; and `/login` / `/signup`
