# Phase P38-e1: Right-size explain runs (plugin 0.9.0)

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

Cut a knowledge:explain run's cost to what the document needs: a delta mode that reuses a recent prior's verified evidence; a research team sized by the document's word budget (top tier for books only); verify only the claims the outline uses; the critic on another model, sampling sources instead of re-opening all; the writer held to its section budgets; a KB resolver that never prints the API key; a metered cost line with a cheaper option in the confirmation. Ships as plugin 0.9.0.

_Durable cross-slice decisions, one gist line each. Replace a superseded line (`--entry K --replace`); never stack versions. Long detail goes to `topics/<slug>.md` behind its gist line (`--to-topic` moves one); a breakdown lives in `topics/breakdown.md`._
- Breakdown (DECOMP): a declared chain of nine slices, S1 to S9, then REVIEW, with no research slice and no DECOMP2. S4 (core invariant: the stage order and resume) and S5 (open design: the delta) are high, the rest low. Each of items 1–11 has one owner; pins are in [topics/breakdown.md](topics/breakdown.md), evidence in [topics/cost-evidence.md](topics/cost-evidence.md).
- No new `## Explain` field and no new `run:` value. The signed sections, the cost line and a delta's decline word ride in the confirmation, which create-phase writes verbatim under `intent.md`'s *Original Input*. The lead reads them there at D0. `outline sign-off` stays a field, with a narrower meaning.
- Every rule slice keeps cited `§` numbers, headings and Part B marker lines byte for byte (new rules go inside existing sections, P35-a4's rule). It also keeps P33-n3's citation check at its baseline `missing=2`: two pre-existing, workspace-side misses (`B5 *Your dispatch*`).
- Sizing calibration from metered runs: a document gets at most 4 researchers (codebase: 2 code + 2 web), a claim cap of the budget's words ÷ 25 (≈ 190 at 4,800), and a document ceiling of 8. The tier table stays for books only. See [topics/cost-evidence.md](topics/cost-evidence.md) §5.
- The KB key travels only in `<KB_EXPLAIN_STATE>/auth.header` (mode 0600, used through `curl -H @file`) or inside the `knowledge` CLI. The resolver prints `KB_API_TOKEN_SET` and `KB_AUTH_HEADER`, never the key. The front door's "write nothing" excepts that one file.
- The loose-document prior lookup uses `knowledge … list --project <p> --limit 200 --json` (the plain text lacks slug and version; `--json` is the server payload verbatim), with `curl -H @<KB_AUTH_HEADER>` as the no-CLI fallback; the prior read is `knowledge … read <rel_path> --source` (P38-e1.S1).
- Verify only what is used (P38-e1.S4): D3v keeps its name but sends no batch (D3s carries the researchers' states as `(reported)`); the pick is **D5p**, the first step of §3.8 (`· used` and `· main`, within `C`, its `D5p` log line written last); D5 verifies only used claims into `verify/final-b<k>.md`; the swap runs in D5s, before D5g, so the gate counts it. A book verifies its main claims before the outline gate; a chapter picks and verifies into its overlay.
- Delta for a v2 (P38-e1.S5): `full` with a `prior:` runs D1d after D0b. It finds the prior's dossier (an explain phase whose `## Published` records that rel_path and version, else a legacy `<KB_EXPLAIN_STATE>/*/response.json`; none: a plain run), copies its evidence to `prior/`, checks sources by content, never claims (code: a diff, then a same-line hash; web: haiku recheck agents re-find passages verbatim), and carries verified claims as `C-p<N>-…` with `reused-from:`. Decline word: `from scratch`.
- Writer and critic (P38-e1.S6): D7 pass 2 holds each section to its budget with one `python3 -c` word count (over 10%: cut before the next); pass 3 cuts nothing. The trace is append-only: a revised line `(rev Cnn)` (`(rev)` in the reader pass), the latest line per sentence key wins, the "In one paragraph" box under key `## top`. B5 trusts verify records, samples ≤ 5 sources; jargon and dense passages are `clarity` should-fixes.
