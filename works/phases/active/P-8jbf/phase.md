# Phase P-8jbf: Wide reader on big screens

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

On PC-width screens the document reader uses the width: above about 1600px the article (header, code, figures, tables) grows from 64rem toward the app's 88rem maximum and paragraph text from 42rem to about 50rem with body text at about 18px; phone, tablet, laptop and print stay as they are.
