# Phase P-8jbf: Wide reader on big screens

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

On PC-width screens the reader uses more of the width: from a 1600px window, documents and book chapters get a wider column (36rem to 44rem) and every text size one step larger (x10/9, default 18px to 20px), with code, tables and figures staying in the column; below 1600px, print and the other reader pages stay as they are (re-targeted after OQ1).
