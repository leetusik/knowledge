# Phase P41-sr: Listen: a free AI voice reader

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

Add a Listen control to the web reader that reads a document, or a whole book chapter after chapter, aloud in English or Korean with a free AI voice (a free API or a free model, chosen by ear from samples), playable on the operator's phone with the screen off; it skips quizzes, code and source lists, and reads a visual's text alternative instead of skipping it when the document has one.

- Spoken script: `server/spoken.py` `spoken_script(tree, lang=, title=)` → `[{section, block, kind, lang, text}]`.
  - **Read:** title, summary, headings, paragraphs, each list item, quote and callout paragraphs (callout
    title first), table body rows as "header: cell; …" (kind `table-row`, so the player can skip them),
    a figure, figures, diagram or svg's alt, label or title then its caption, and a formula's alt,
    reading and "sym: means".
  - **Skipped:** code, hr, quiz, sources, glossary.
  - **Inline:** math becomes linear text; an inline image reads its alt (P41-sr.S1).
- Playback shape (R3): one continuous media source per Listen, with chapters as seeks inside it. Never
  swap `src` at `ended`, which fails on a locked iPhone (WebKit 173332, 261554, 261858). See
  `topics/phone-playback.md` §3.

_Questions only the operator can answer, one entry each: `- **OQ<n> (<slice>):** <question>`. - **OQ1 (P41-sr.R1):** The voice pick hinges on two things:
  1. May Listen rely on an unofficial endpoint? edge-tts is Edge's Read Aloud service, with no published
     terms.
  2. May your private document text go to a free tier that trains on it and allows human review? That
     covers Gemini's free tier, and Fish's "may improve models".

  If neither is allowed, only Deepgram (a training opt-out and a finite credit) and local models remain.
  See `topics/voice-apis.md` § Risks.
- **OQ2 (P41-sr.R3):** Beside the web player, do you want a private podcast feed for books?
  - **What you would get:** listening in a podcast app, offline, with the most reliable locked playback.
  - **The cost:** its secret URL reaches the app's servers, and with Overcast the audio does too.
  - **Recommendation:** not now. File it as a deferred job, and build it if the web player fails your
    iPhone check. See `topics/phone-playback.md` §8.
- **OQ3 (P41-sr.R2):** The shared Oracle box:
  - How many OCPUs and how much RAM does it have, and how much of each is free beside your other apps?
  - Why it matters: the light voices need about 1 free core and 1.5 GB of RAM to render a book in a few
    hours in the background.
  - May an agent run one 10-second timing test on it, so the projection becomes a measurement?
