## Why one slice, not two

- this repo's `plugin/skills/explain/SKILL.md`: 158 KB
- the bootstrap repo's `payload/contract.md`, `create-phase/SKILL.md`, `do-whole-phase/SKILL.md` and the three `explain-*.md` references: 84 KB together

- the engine, `payload/scripts/workflow.py`: 850 KB, with 11 `explain` hits

S1 needs only named sections and grep hits, not whole files, and one high-row context with `Explore` can hold that. The plan's suggested split (plugin analysis first) would push the plugin side through a findings topic. The handoff's central judgment, which side owns what, is made by comparing the two repos directly, so I kept the job in one slice.
