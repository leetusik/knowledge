- **Reader chrome rules amended by the operator at the walkthrough (P30.F5, 2026-10-21):** the operator's words ("make it invisible when scrolling down, shows up when only user tries to tab the screen or up scrolling"; "the bottom bar that comes up when I do even very slight scroll up… super annoying"; "it's not hid when I see in pc") supersede the signed hide/show tables of `01-reader` r02 and `09-legacy-and-print` r01. Pure rules in `web/src/lib/reader/chrome.ts`; detail OQ185–OQ190.
- **The reader owns its landing (P30.F6):** every page it opens lands by `landingFor` (hash, `?note=`, resume, top) and writes nothing until `landingSettled` (`web/src/lib/reader/position.ts`). Next's scroll-to-top is not relied on: its handler takes the segment's first node, the reader stylesheet React hoists into `<head>`, and gives up. `experimental.appNewScrollHandler` was not turned on app-wide (wider blast radius than the reader).
