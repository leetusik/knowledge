Hermes stays on the compose network; none of its own text reaches readers. → [topics/hermes-fit.md](topics/hermes-fit.md) § Q5
- Saved chats and spend (S1, corrected live): Postgres is the source of truth; Hermes sessions are a cache keyed by thread id, re-seeded with `conversation_history` if lost or after a failed run. Spend is priced per provider call from the guard plugin's tap (cache split); `run.completed.usage` has none and overstates 1.5–10×; a refused run is unmetered, so it is charged a flat allowance. → [topics/hermes-fit.md](topics/hermes-fit.md) § Effort and usage
