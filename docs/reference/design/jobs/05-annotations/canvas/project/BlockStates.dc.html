Component — Notes and bookmarks on typed blocks

A typed block (a diagram, a quiz, a figure, the sources) is a section of its own when it has an id: its title or caption takes a note on it and a bookmark, never a highlight. The diagram is the reader’s canvas-only stand-in; quiz, figure and sources are stand-ins naming their jobs, to show where the marker and "Bookmarked" sit. Text before the first section takes nothing yet (Q5).
