Component — Place bar, undo line, sign-in prompt

The bottom of the reading screen holds one thing at a time, over the chapter bar. The place bar names the section you are in and takes a note on it or a bookmark; in a plain Markdown document it bookmarks the position. After a delete the undo line says what went (the review card included) and offers Undo for ten seconds. Signed out, a bookmark asks to sign in with the shared prompt, its noun changed to bookmarks or highlights and notes.
