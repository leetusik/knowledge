Component — Figure

A figure is the image, its caption and its credit, read as one. The image is shown at its own pixel size or smaller, never scaled up, on a hairline edge, in a box reserved at its proportions. The caption sits under it in the interface face at the column's small step, in secondary; the credit has its own line under a CREDIT label. No figure numbers. Light, dark and sepia leave the image's pixels alone.
