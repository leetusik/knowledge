Component — The document bar (LegacyBar)

The bar under the frame in the legacy reader: the document’s status, Print · new tab, the signed bookmark control and its line, and two short notes: no highlights here, and the theme and text size don’t restyle it. At the end of a document its status turns into the signed chapter end’s words in one grid cell, so the bar keeps one height. A notice sits on top when the version or the translation is older. Hover and focus are reached on the Pages (01 and 03); a press there toggles Bookmarked here.
